# TFNE Column Ontology

## Core Terms

- **Mollified Source**: A spatial source distribution $q(x)$ derived from a point current $I$ using a smooth kernel $\eta(x)$ such that $\int q(x) dx = I$.
- **Gauge Fix**: The constraint applied to the extracellular potential $\phi_e$ (e.g., mean-zero) to ensure a unique solution to the Poisson equation.
- **Resistive Conduction**: Extracellular current density $J = -\sigma_e \nabla \phi_e$ where $\sigma_e$ is a frequency-independent scalar conductivity.
- **Synaptic Delay**: The discrete time interval between a presynaptic spike event and its corresponding postsynaptic conductance change.
- **LFP Proxy**: A sampled or averaged extracellular potential $\phi_e$ at specific electrode coordinates.
